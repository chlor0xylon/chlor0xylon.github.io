title: ImageNet hierarchy graph
description: Interactive view of the WordNet hierarchy condensed onto the 1000 ImageNet classes.
---

The WordNet hierarchy condensed onto the 1000 ImageNet classes. Drag to pan, scroll to zoom, hover a node to see its label, or look one up with the search box. Node colors come from the spectral embedding described in [the article](/posts/imagenet-hidden-hierarchy/).
